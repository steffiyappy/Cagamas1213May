Business & Client Coverage Build Pack

🤖 Agent: "HHFC Lender Connect"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Business & Client Coverage division manages relationships with 60+ financial institution partners. The team handles lender onboarding, portfolio purchase processing, and ongoing client servicing. Relationship managers spend significant time answering routine lender queries that could be handled by a well-designed AI agent.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Lender Connect

Purpose: Help HHFC's external lender partners get quick answers about onboarding requirements, submission status, and HHFC products — reducing the volume of routine queries hitting relationship managers.

Primary Users: Lender representatives at financial institutions (banks, DFIs, cooperatives) who regularly interact with HHFC.

Core Problem it Solves: Currently, routine queries consume 2–6 hours per interaction per relationship manager. An agent that can handle the most common 70% of queries would free up RMs to focus on high-value relationship work and complex negotiations.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1 — Onboarding Requirements
What documents do I need to submit a new mortgage portfolio to HHFC?
🤖 HHFC Lender Connect To submit a conventional mortgage portfolio to HHFC, you will need: (1) Completed Portfolio Submission Form (PSF-01), (2) Loan schedule in HHFC standard template, (3) Current valuation reports for all properties, (4) Copy of executed facility agreements, (5) Originator declaration and representations. For Islamic portfolios (PWR-i / PWOR-i), you will also need the Shariah compliance declaration. Shall I connect you with your assigned Relationship Manager for a submission checklist walkthrough?
Scenario 2 — Product Information
What is the difference between PWR and PWOR?
🤖 HHFC Lender Connect Great question. Both are HHFC's conventional mortgage purchase products:

PWR (Purchase with Recourse): HHFC purchases the mortgage assets but retains recourse to the originating institution if loans default. Lower risk transfer for the lender.

PWOR (Purchase Without Recourse): HHFC purchases the assets and bears the credit risk. Higher upfront pricing but full risk transfer to HHFC.

Islamic equivalents are PWR-i and PWOR-i. Would you like me to explain the Mortgage Guarantee Programme (MGP) as well?
Scenario 3 — Submission Status
We submitted a portfolio last Tuesday. What's the status?
🤖 HHFC Lender Connect I'd be happy to help you check on that. Could you provide your submission reference number (format: SUB-YYYY-XXXXX) and your institution's name? I don't have live access to the processing system, but I can escalate to your assigned Relationship Manager immediately who will respond within 2 business hours. Alternatively, I can send a status request on your behalf right now.
Scenario 4 — Pricing Query
What is HHFC's current purchase price for a standard 30-year home loan?
🤖 HHFC Lender Connect HHFC's purchase pricing is indicative and set based on prevailing market rates, loan characteristics, and portfolio composition at the time of submission. I'm not able to provide binding pricing commitments through this channel. For the latest indicative term sheet and pricing guidance, I'll connect you to your assigned Relationship Manager. Would you like me to do that now?

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define your agent's scope, persona, and name. Configure its system prompt so it understands it is HHFC's Lender Relationship Assistant and knows the boundaries of what it can and cannot answer (e.g., it cannot give pricing commitments or access live submission data).
  2. Build at least 3 topics: (a) Onboarding Requirements, (b) Product Information (PWR vs PWOR vs MGP), (c) Submission Status & Escalation. For each topic, write the trigger phrases and response content.
  3. Test your agent against all 4 sample conversations above. Identify at least one gap in your agent's knowledge and show how you fixed it before the presentation.
