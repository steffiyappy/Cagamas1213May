Business & Client Coverage Build Pack

🤖 Agent: "HHFC Lender Connect"
⏱️ Half-Day Sprint: You have 90 minutes to build your agent.
Focus on getting ONE agent topic working well rather than building everything.

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Business & Client Coverage division manages relationships with more than 60 lender partners across the market. The team supports onboarding, mortgage portfolio submissions, and day-to-day partner queries, so a simple self-service agent can remove a lot of repetitive back-and-forth.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Lender Connect

What it does: Answers routine lender queries about onboarding requirements and HHFC mortgage products so relationship managers spend less time repeating the same explanations.

Who uses it: Lender representatives at banks, DFIs, and cooperatives who work with HHFC on mortgage purchases.

Core problem: Basic questions about documents, products, and next steps still take up RM time that should be spent on relationship building and complex cases.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1: Onboarding Requirements
What documents do I need to submit a new mortgage portfolio to HHFC?
🤖 HHFC Lender Connect To submit a new mortgage portfolio, please prepare these 5 core documents: PSF-01 form, loan schedule, valuation reports, facility agreements, and the originator declaration. If this is an Islamic portfolio, include the Shariah compliance declaration as well. Would you like me to connect you with your Relationship Manager for a submission checklist walkthrough?
Scenario 2: Product Info: PWR vs PWOR
What is the difference between PWR and PWOR?
🤖 HHFC Lender Connect PWR means Purchase with Recourse, where HHFC buys the mortgage assets but retains recourse to the originating lender for defaults. PWOR means Purchase Without Recourse, where HHFC buys the assets and takes on the credit risk. If you need help choosing the right structure, I can connect you with your Relationship Manager.

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define the Agent Identity (~20 min): Your agent needs an identity before it can do anything. What is it called, what does it know, and what should it never say? Think from a lender's perspective and ask yourself: would they trust it? Goal: Get your agent created and configured with a clear purpose.
  2. Build the Core Topic (~40 min): Lenders have questions, and your agent needs one answer flow that saves relationship managers real time. Pick the topic with the biggest payoff, then shape trigger phrases, a helpful response, and a natural handoff. Goal: Get one complete, working topic into your agent.
  3. Pressure-Test & Refine (~20 min): Now think like a real lender and ask the kind of question that shows whether your agent is ready. Does the response feel useful, trustworthy, and on-brand? Goal: Find one thing to improve, and fix it before you present.

💡 M365 Copilot Agent Builder Tips

  • Start at: microsoft365.com/copilot - then click the Copilot icon, go to Agents, and select Create agent
  • Click Agents (top right) → Create agent, then give it a name and description
  • Use "Topics" to add conversation flows. Each topic needs trigger phrases and a response
  • Click "Test your agent" (right panel) to chat with it and see if it works
  • Keep responses short and helpful. Bullet points work better than long paragraphs
