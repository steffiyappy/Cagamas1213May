Islamic Finance Build Pack

🤖 Agent: "HHFC Shariah Guide"
⏱️ Half-Day Sprint: You have 90 minutes to build your agent.
Focus on getting ONE agent topic working well rather than building everything.

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Islamic Finance division manages Shariah-compliant mortgage products and works closely with lenders on Islamic submissions. The team often repeats the same explanations on product structure and supporting documents, making it a strong fit for a focused guidance agent.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Shariah Guide

What it does: Helps lender partners understand Shariah compliance requirements for HHFC's Islamic mortgage products, especially PWR-i and PWOR-i.

Who uses it: Relationship teams and lender representatives preparing Islamic mortgage submissions.

Core problem: Routine questions about product differences and required declarations still depend on manual follow-up from the Islamic Finance team.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1: Shariah Compliance Documents
What documents do I need for an Islamic submission?
🤖 HHFC Shariah Guide For an Islamic submission, please include the standard portfolio documents plus the Shariah compliance declaration and any supporting product documentation required by HHFC. I can also share the Shariah declaration requirements if you are preparing a PWR-i or PWOR-i submission.
Scenario 2: Product Differences
What is the difference between PWR-i and PWOR-i?
🤖 HHFC Shariah Guide PWR-i is Islamic Purchase with Recourse, while PWOR-i is Islamic Purchase Without Recourse. Both are Shariah-compliant mortgage purchase products, but the recourse structure is different. Final Shariah rulings should always be confirmed with HHFC's Shariah Supervisory Council.

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define the Agent Identity (~20 min): Your agent is HHFC's Islamic finance assistant, so confidence matters from the first answer. What should it always know, and what should it always clarify before a lender relies on it? Goal: Get your agent created with a clear Islamic finance persona.
  2. Build the Core Topic (~40 min): The most common question about Islamic products is often the one that causes the most confusion. Choose that question and build a topic that answers it clearly, correctly, and without jargon. Goal: Get one complete, working topic into your agent.
  3. Pressure-Test & Refine (~20 min): Test your agent the way a sceptical lender would: ask something slightly tricky and see how it behaves. Does it stay clear, safe, and honest about its limits? Goal: Identify one weakness and fix it before you present.
