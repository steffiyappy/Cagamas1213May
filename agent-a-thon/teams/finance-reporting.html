Finance & Reporting Build Pack

🤖 Agent: "HHFC Finance Desk"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Finance division manages financial planning and Group performance reporting. Monthly Management Accounts Pack production currently takes 6 days and results in rushed commentary. An agent that helps management teams query financial data and draft variance commentary could fundamentally change the Finance team's capacity for value-add analysis.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Finance Desk

Purpose: Help HHFC staff query financial performance data, understand variances, and get guided assistance on budget vs actual — reducing the volume of ad-hoc finance queries hitting the Finance team and giving management faster access to financial context.

Primary Users: Cost centre heads, senior managers, and the Finance team itself during month-end reporting cycles.

Core Problem it Solves: Cost centre heads frequently contact Finance with basic queries that consume team time. An agent that handles routine lookups and helps draft commentary would free Finance to focus on analysis and the CFO's strategic questions.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1 — Performance Summary
What was HHFC's total interest income in Q1 2025?
🤖 HHFC Finance Desk Based on the Management Accounts data, HHFC's reported interest income for Q1 2025 was approximately RM 408.9M (following an accrual timing adjustment from an initial RM 412.3M). The key driver was coupon income from the conventional mortgage portfolio. For a full breakdown by product line, the Management Pack Q1 section provides the detailed schedule. Would you like me to explain the main variance drivers or help you draft commentary for a specific cost centre?
Scenario 2 — Variance Explanation
Why was the Islamic Finance sukuk revenue higher than budget in Q2?
🤖 HHFC Finance Desk The Islamic Finance sukuk revenue variance in Q2 was driven by a fee income reclassification — specifically, certain structuring fees that were initially categorised under advisory income were reclassified to sukuk revenue, resulting in an upward revision from RM 188.4M to RM 191.2M. This is a presentation adjustment rather than an underlying performance change. For future reporting clarity, Finance is standardising the fee income classification treatment across all cost centres. Would you like me to draft the variance commentary paragraph for this line item?
Scenario 3 — Commentary Drafting
I need to write the variance commentary for the Treasury cost centre. Can you help?
🤖 HHFC Finance Desk Of course. To draft accurate commentary, could you confirm: (1) the actual vs budget variance amount, (2) the primary driver (e.g., issuance timing, rate movement, volume), and (3) whether the variance is expected to reverse in H2? Once you provide those details, I'll generate a commentary paragraph in standard Management Accounts style — concise, factual, and ready for Finance review before inclusion in the pack.
Scenario 4 — OPEX Status
What is our current year-to-date OPEX versus budget?
🤖 HHFC Finance Desk Based on the latest Management Accounts data, HHFC's YTD OPEX shows the IT & Operations cost centre as the most notable over-budget item (RM 9.7M actual vs RM 8.2M budget — a +18% variance) driven by licence renewal costs. The Risk & Compliance provision is also above initial estimates. For the most current YTD position, I recommend confirming with the Finance team as figures may have been updated since the last pack. Would you like me to help format this into a brief OPEX summary for your cost centre review?

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Configure the Finance Desk agent with appropriate knowledge boundaries — it can help interpret and summarise financial data, and assist with commentary drafting, but must direct users to Finance for data validation and final approval of any figures.
  2. Build topics for: (a) Performance Summary Q&A, (b) Variance Commentary Drafting Assistant, (c) Budget vs Actual Query, (d) Board Pack Navigation Help. Write triggers and responses for each.
  3. Test all 4 conversations. For the commentary drafting topic, demonstrate that the agent produces commentary in the correct financial reporting style — concise, structured, and variance-focused.
