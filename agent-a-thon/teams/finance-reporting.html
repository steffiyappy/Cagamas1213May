🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Finance & Reporting division manages management accounts, reporting packs, and month-end coordination. Staff frequently answer repeat questions on deadlines and commentary expectations, which makes a simple reporting assistant very practical for a half-day build.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Finance Desk

What it does: Helps finance staff quickly find reporting templates, deadlines, and standard variance commentary guidance.

Who uses it: Finance team members, cost centre owners, and managers preparing month-end or quarterly submissions.

Core problem: Finance spends too much time repeating timelines and explaining how to write basic variance notes instead of focusing on analysis.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1: Reporting Deadline
When is the next board pack due?
🤖 HHFC Finance Desk HHFC's standard reporting schedule is: monthly management accounts by the 5th working day, quarterly board pack by the 15th of month-end, and annual statutory accounts by 31 March. If you need an extension, please contact the Finance Controller.
Scenario 2: Variance Explanation
Help me explain a budget variance.
🤖 HHFC Finance Desk A simple variance commentary template is: "Revenue was [higher/lower] than budget by RM[X] due to [reason]. This was [partially/fully] offset by [factor]. Management expects [outlook]." If you give me the amount and driver, I can help you draft it more clearly.

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define the Agent Identity (~10 min): Finance people trust tools that are precise about what they can and cannot do. Shape your agent so it feels reliable, disciplined, and clear about where live data or human judgement still matters. Goal: Get your agent created with a clear, dependable Finance persona.
  2. Build the Core Topic (~25 min): Reporting deadlines create pressure, and your agent should remove some of it. Choose the one topic that would help analysts most during crunch time and build it well. Goal: Get one complete, working topic into your agent.
  3. Pressure-Test & Refine (~10 min): Test it like a stressed Finance analyst at month-end. Is the answer fast, clear, and useful enough when time is tight? Goal: Make one improvement that would matter most under pressure.

💡 M365 Copilot Agent Builder Tips

  • Start at: microsoft365.com/copilot - then click the Copilot icon, go to Agents, and select Create agent
  • Click Agents (top right) → Create agent, then give it a name and description
  • Use "Topics" to add conversation flows. Each topic needs trigger phrases and a response
  • Click "Test your agent" (right panel) to chat with it and see if it works
  • Keep responses short and helpful. Bullet points work better than long paragraphs
