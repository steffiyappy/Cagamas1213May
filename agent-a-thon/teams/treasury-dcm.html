🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Treasury & Capital Markets division manages funding, bond issuance, and investor communication. The team regularly responds to repeat questions on bond programmes and market context, so a fast internal assistant can save time during busy issuance periods.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Market Desk

What it does: Helps treasury staff quickly access bond issuance summaries, market briefings, and standard investor answers.

Who uses it: Internal Treasury users first, with content that can also support investor-facing FAQs.

Core problem: Staff need fast answers during issuance windows, but they should not have to rebuild the same overview every time someone asks about programmes or rates.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1: Bond Issuance Status
Tell me about HHFC's latest bond programme.
🤖 HHFC Market Desk HHFC issues conventional bonds and sukuk under its RM50 billion Medium Term Notes programme. These issuances are used to fund mortgage asset purchases. For the latest indicative rates or launch timing, please check the Bloomberg terminal or contact the Debt Capital Markets team.
Scenario 2: Market Rate Query
What is the current MGS rate?
🤖 HHFC Market Desk I can help with general market context, but I do not provide binding live market data. Please check the Bloomberg terminal for the latest MGS rate, or contact the Treasury desk if you need a current market snapshot.

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define the Agent Identity (~10 min): Treasury teams work in precision and speed, so your agent needs a sharp boundary around what it knows and what it redirects. Configure its identity with that reality in mind. Goal: Get your agent created with a sharp, focused persona.
  2. Build the Core Topic (~25 min): Treasury keeps getting the same questions, and one of them wastes more time than the rest. Pick that high-friction topic and build a response flow that handles it properly. Goal: Get one complete, working topic into your agent.
  3. Pressure-Test & Refine (~10 min): Ask your agent something right at the edge of what it should know. Does it stay useful without pretending certainty? Goal: Find one gap and close it before you present.

💡 M365 Copilot Agent Builder Tips

  • Start at: microsoft365.com/copilot - then click the Copilot icon, go to Agents, and select Create agent
  • Click Agents (top right) → Create agent, then give it a name and description
  • Use "Topics" to add conversation flows. Each topic needs trigger phrases and a response
  • Click "Test your agent" (right panel) to chat with it and see if it works
  • Keep responses short and helpful. Bullet points work better than long paragraphs
