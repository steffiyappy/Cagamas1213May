Risk & Compliance Build Pack

🤖 Agent: "HHFC Compliance Radar"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Risk & Compliance division monitors ~8 regulatory circulars per month from BNM, SC, and AAOIFI. The team spends 14 hours per week on manual monitoring and tracking. Two compliance breaches were only caught at quarterly review. With a BNM examiner visit approaching, the team needs to move from reactive to proactive compliance monitoring.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Compliance Radar

Purpose: Help the Risk & Compliance team and business unit managers quickly understand new regulatory circulars, assess preliminary impact on HHFC's operations, and stay on top of open compliance items — reducing the 14 hours per week spent on manual monitoring.

Primary Users: Compliance officers, Risk team members, and business unit heads who receive regulatory circulars and need rapid preliminary assessments.

Core Problem it Solves: The gap between receiving a circular and producing a structured impact assessment is currently weeks. An agent that can generate a structured preliminary assessment in minutes changes the team's capacity for real-time monitoring.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1 — New Circular Summary
BNM just issued a new circular on outsourcing risk management. What does HHFC need to check?
🤖 HHFC Compliance Radar BNM's Outsourcing Risk Management guidelines typically require financial institutions to assess five operational areas. For HHFC specifically, I recommend checking:

1. Third-party vendor contracts — Do all material outsourcing arrangements have BNM-compliant terms (right of audit, data protection, exit provisions)?
2. Core system dependencies — Is the mortgage processing system hosted externally? If so, review against the guidelines' materiality threshold.
3. Cloud service agreements — Microsoft 365 and any Azure services should be reviewed against the data residency requirements.
4. Business continuity provisions — Do outsourced functions have tested BCPs acceptable under the new guidance?
5. Board oversight evidence — Does the Board receive an outsourcing risk report as required?

⚠️ This is a preliminary assessment. Please review the full circular and confirm with your qualified compliance officer.
Scenario 2 — Open Item Status
What is our current compliance status on the Green Bond Disclosure Requirements from SC?
🤖 HHFC Compliance Radar Based on the regulatory monitoring log, SC-2025-012 (Green Bond Disclosure Requirements) is currently marked: Relevant: Yes | Impact Assessment: Partial | Remediation Required: Yes | Status: Pending.

This circular has been in the log since March 2025. The partial impact assessment indicates that some review has been done but remediation actions have not been formally assigned. Given that HHFC issued a Green Bond in May 2025 (ISS-003), this circular is likely directly applicable to your existing disclosure documents. I recommend: (1) completing the full impact assessment, (2) reviewing the May 2025 green bond prospectus disclosure against the new requirements, (3) assigning a named remediation owner with a deadline. Would you like me to draft a preliminary impact assessment for this circular?
Scenario 3 — AAOIFI Standard Query
What is AAOIFI Shariah Standard 59 and does it affect our sukuk issuances?
🤖 HHFC Compliance Radar AAOIFI Shariah Standard 59 governs the Shariah requirements for Sale and Leaseback transactions, which are commonly used as the underlying structure in certain sukuk. Key requirements include: (1) the sale must be genuine and not merely a financing device; (2) the leaseback rental must reflect market rates; (3) buy-back options must be structured carefully to avoid creating a guaranteed return.

For HHFC's sukuk programme, Standard 59 is relevant if any of your sukuk structures use sale-leaseback or Ijarah elements. I recommend the Islamic Finance team review current issuance structures against Standard 59's updated requirements. This should be on the SAC's agenda for the next quarterly meeting. ⚠️ Confirmed applicability requires review by your Shariah Advisor.
Scenario 4 — Impact Assessment Draft
Can you draft a preliminary impact assessment for the new BNM cyber risk guidelines?
🤖 HHFC Compliance Radar Here is a preliminary impact assessment for BNM-2025-028 (Cyber Risk Guidelines):

1. Summary: BNM's Cyber Risk Guidelines establish mandatory cyber risk governance standards for all licensed financial institutions, including requirements for Board-level cyber risk oversight, third-party cyber risk management, and incident reporting timelines.
2. Applicability to HHFC: High. As a BNM-licensed financial institution, HHFC is fully subject to these guidelines.
3. Identified Gaps (Preliminary): (a) Board-level cyber risk dashboard may need formalising; (b) Third-party vendor cyber assessments may need updating; (c) Incident reporting procedures should be tested against the new 4-hour notification timeline.
4. Required Actions: Cyber risk policy review, vendor assessment update, Board reporting enhancement.
5. Suggested Timeline: 90 days for full gap assessment, 180 days for full remediation.

⚠️ This is a preliminary assessment for planning purposes only. All assessments must be validated by a qualified compliance professional.
