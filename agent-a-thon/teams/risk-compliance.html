🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Risk & Compliance division tracks regulatory updates, compliance obligations, and internal review checklists. The team needs quick, consistent guidance on what to check first when a new BNM or compliance question comes in.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC Compliance Radar

What it does: Helps compliance staff quickly access regulatory update summaries and practical compliance checklists.

Who uses it: Risk and Compliance team members, control owners, and colleagues preparing internal submissions.

Core problem: New regulatory questions often trigger repeated manual explanations, even when the first response is largely standard.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1: BNM Guideline Query
Are there any new BNM guidelines I should know about?
🤖 HHFC Compliance Radar Key recent areas to watch include Climate Risk disclosure requirements under BNM's Climate Change & Principle-based Taxonomy, updates to the Capital Adequacy Framework, and PDPA-related obligations for customer data handling. For the full text, visit bnm.gov.my or contact the Compliance team.
Scenario 2: Compliance Checklist
What do I need to check before submission?
🤖 HHFC Compliance Radar Use this 5-point pre-submission check: (1) data classification confirmed, (2) PDPA consent obtained, (3) AML screening completed, (4) counterparty sanctions check done, (5) risk committee sign-off obtained. For legal interpretation, please escalate to the Legal & Compliance team.

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define the Agent Identity (~10 min): Compliance agents carry real responsibility, so credibility matters immediately. Decide what yours covers, what it flags, and what it should always refer onward before anyone relies on it too far. Goal: Get your agent created with a serious, trustworthy compliance persona.
  2. Build the Core Topic (~25 min): Regulatory questions are repetitive, stressful, and high-stakes. Pick one high-frequency question and build a topic that answers it clearly, correctly, and with the right boundaries. Goal: Get one complete, working topic into your agent.
  3. Pressure-Test & Refine (~10 min): Test the agent with a question that would matter if it got the answer wrong. Does it respond well, and does it know when to escalate? Goal: Make one refinement that increases your confidence in the agent's reliability.

💡 M365 Copilot Agent Builder Tips

  • Start at: microsoft365.com/copilot - then click the Copilot icon, go to Agents, and select Create agent
  • Click Agents (top right) → Create agent, then give it a name and description
  • Use "Topics" to add conversation flows. Each topic needs trigger phrases and a response
  • Click "Test your agent" (right panel) to chat with it and see if it works
  • Keep responses short and helpful. Bullet points work better than long paragraphs
