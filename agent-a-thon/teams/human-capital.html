Human Capital Build Pack

🤖 Agent: "HHFC People Connect"
⏱️ Half-Day Sprint: You have 90 minutes to build your agent.
Focus on getting ONE agent topic working well rather than building everything.

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Human Capital division supports HR policy, recruitment, and employee development across the organisation. A self-service people agent can quickly answer repeat questions on leave, training, and general processes so HR can focus on more sensitive matters.

🤖 Agent Brief: HHFC People Connect

What it does: Helps employees find answers to common HR policy questions without needing to call HR for every routine query.

Who uses it: HHFC employees and managers looking for guidance on leave, benefits, training, and general HR processes.

Core problem: Repeat policy questions take time away from HR Business Partners, even when the answer is already standard and well-defined.

💬 Sample Conversations

Scenario 1: Leave Policy
How many days annual leave do I get?
🤖 HHFC People Connect HHFC leave entitlements are: 20 days annual leave for executives, 25 days for managers and above, 14 days outpatient medical leave, 60 days medical leave with hospitalisation, and 3 days emergency leave. For leave applications, please log in to the HRMS portal.
Scenario 2: Training Request
How do I apply for external training?
🤖 HHFC People Connect To apply for external training: (1) get line manager approval, (2) submit the training nomination form to Human Capital, and (3) HC will process it within 5 working days. If your request is urgent or unusual, please contact your HR Business Partner.

🔧 Build Tasks

  1. Define the Agent Identity (~20 min): HR agents need to feel human before they feel useful. Configure yours so employees would trust it with everyday questions and still know when it should hand off something sensitive. Goal: Get your agent created with a warm, employee-friendly persona.
  2. Build the Core Topic (~40 min): Leave and benefits questions come up constantly, but one topic probably creates the most repeat traffic. Choose it and build a response flow that makes people feel informed instead of confused. Goal: Get one complete, working topic into your agent.
  3. Pressure-Test & Refine (~20 min): Test your agent like a new employee who does not know the policy language yet. Where could the answer still confuse, frustrate, or mislead someone? Goal: Find one thing that could confuse a user and fix it.

💡 M365 Copilot Agent Builder Tips

  • Start at: microsoft365.com/copilot - then click the Copilot icon, go to Agents, and select Create agent
  • Click Agents (top right) → Create agent, then give it a name and description
  • Use "Topics" to add conversation flows. Each topic needs trigger phrases and a response
  • Click "Test your agent" (right panel) to chat with it and see if it works
  • Keep responses short and helpful. Bullet points work better than long paragraphs
