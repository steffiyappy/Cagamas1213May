Strategy & Research Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Insight Time Lag"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Strategy & Research division drives the corporation's strategic planning cycle, produces quarterly housing market research reports, manages ESG strategy and reporting, and provides the analytical foundation for major Board decisions. The team of 8 analysts and strategists is small relative to the scope of the work.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: HHFC's flagship Quarterly Housing Market Outlook report — 60–80 pages — takes 3 weeks to produce. By the time it reaches the Board, market conditions have frequently moved. In Q1 2025, BNM announced an OPR change 2 weeks after the report was submitted for approval — a change the report had not considered. The CSO has committed to producing monthly briefs by Q4 2025.

Data is sourced manually from NAPIC, BNM, DOSM, and other government portals — downloaded, cleaned, and merged in Excel. Compilation involves 4–5 revision rounds. The CSO's commitment to monthly briefs would require producing credible content in 5–7 working days, down from the current 21 days.

📧 CSO Email

From: Nurul Ain Razali (Chief Strategy Officer) To: Head of Research; Strategy Team Subject: Monthly Briefs — The Commitment Is Made I committed to the Board last month that we would move to monthly research briefs by Q4 2025. I made that commitment because I believe it is the right thing for HHFC strategically. I also know that at our current pace, we cannot do it. Three weeks for a quarterly report means we would need to produce something credible in 5–7 working days for a monthly brief. That requires us to work fundamentally differently — not just faster. I need ideas on how we get there. What data pipelines can we establish? What can AI tools do to accelerate our synthesis and drafting? What does a 'good enough' monthly brief look like versus our current 80-page quarterly? Let's solve this together.

🎯 Your Tasks

  1. Analyse the research production log and identify the specific stages where time is being lost — distinguish between data collection, analysis, writing, and review phases.
  2. Redesign HHFC's research production process to meet the CSO's commitment: a credible monthly housing market brief produced in 5–7 days. Describe what changes structurally and how Copilot supports each phase.
  3. Draft a concise 2-page "Monthly Housing Market Flash Brief" for Q2 2025, using available knowledge of the Malaysian housing market to make it realistic — demonstrate what the new format should look like.
