Business & Client Coverage Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Lender Labyrinth"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Business & Client Coverage division manages the corporation's relationships with 60+ financial institution partners (banks, development financial institutions, cooperatives). The team handles lender onboarding, portfolio purchase processing, and ongoing client servicing, which is the lifeblood of HHFC's mortgage purchase operations.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: HHFC's lender onboarding process involves 12 manual touchpoints averaging 18 days per transaction, well above the industry norm of 5 days. Two mid-sized banks have begun shifting mortgage portfolio sales to a competitor. HHFC's volume from these institutions fell 23% in H1.

Client history, correspondence, and portfolio preferences are spread across individual email inboxes, shared drives with no consistent naming convention, and personal notebooks. When a key relationship manager resigned in Q3, HHFC's largest new lender account sat unserviced for 11 days while a replacement was briefed from scratch.

🎯 Your Tasks

Three focused Copilot tasks. Each one produces a real output you'll present at the end. Aim for ~25 minutes per task.

  1. Analyse & Summarise the Problem⏱️ ~25 min
    The lender interaction log is telling a story. Uncover it. What's consuming the most time, and who's overstretched? Deliver: a 4-bullet executive summary of your key findings.
  2. Draft a Recovery Email⏱️ ~25 min
    Bank Setia is drifting away, and someone needs to win them back. Think about what a senior lender actually wants to hear from HHFC right now: ownership, specifics, and confidence. Deliver: a draft response email under 200 words.
  3. Build the EXCO Talking Points⏱️ ~25 min
    You're presenting to EXCO in 30 minutes. What are the 3 things leaders need to know and act on first, and how would you frame them as Problem, Cause, Fix? Deliver: 3 leadership-ready talking points.
