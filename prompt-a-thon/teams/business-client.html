⚡ Challenge: "The Lender Labyrinth"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Business & Client Coverage division manages the corporation's relationships with 60+ financial institution partners (banks, development financial institutions, cooperatives). The team handles lender onboarding, portfolio purchase processing, and ongoing client servicing — the lifeblood of HHFC's mortgage purchase operations.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: HHFC's lender onboarding process involves 12 manual touchpoints averaging 18 days per transaction — well above the industry norm of 5 days. Two mid-sized banks have begun shifting mortgage portfolio sales to a competitor. HHFC's volume from these institutions fell 23% in H1.

Client history, correspondence, and portfolio preferences are spread across individual email inboxes, shared drives with no consistent naming convention, and personal notebooks. When a key relationship manager resigned in Q3, HHFC's largest new lender account sat unserviced for 11 days while a replacement was briefed from scratch.
