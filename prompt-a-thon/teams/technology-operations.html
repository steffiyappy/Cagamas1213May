Technology & Operations Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Legacy Bottleneck"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Technology & Operations division manages the corporation's core mortgage processing systems, IT infrastructure, and operational workflows. The team is responsible for system change management, digital transformation under Harmoni Vision 2027, and ensuring that day-to-day processing (mortgage purchase settlement, payment processing, securities issuance) runs without disruption.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: HHFC's core mortgage processing system requires a BRD and formal UAT sign-off for every change. The current queue stands at 23 pending change requests, with an average change cycle of 6–8 weeks. Two critical processes run on manual workarounds — three operations staff spend 2 hours each morning manually extracting data the system should produce automatically.

Each BRD averages 47 pages and takes a senior business analyst 3–4 days to write. UAT test scripts must be written manually against each BRD. The Head of Business & Client Coverage wrote: "We are asking our people to work around the system every single day. This is not acceptable for an institution of our standing."
