Islamic Finance Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Shariah Checklist Crunch"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Islamic Finance division manages the purchase of Islamic mortgage assets and the issuance of Shariah-compliant sukuk. The division works closely with the Shariah Advisory Committee to ensure all products meet Islamic finance principles. Every sukuk issuance requires a full Shariah compliance package — and preparing it is taking too long.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Every sukuk issuance requires the team to manually cross-check 140+ Shariah compliance items across Arabic fatwas and English prospectuses. This takes 3–4 working days. In 2 of the last 6 issuances, the Shariah Advisory Committee found discrepancies during final review — causing 3–5 day delays and extra legal costs.

The team needs a faster, more reliable way to identify the highest-risk review items, standardise the checklist, and improve pre-submission quality before the next issuance cycle begins.

🎯 Your Tasks

Three focused Copilot tasks. Each one produces a real output you'll present at the end. Aim for ~25 minutes per task.

  1. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 1
    Identify the Risk Items
    Open Copilot Chat. Paste the Shariah compliance checklist log and ask: "Based on this checklist log, which items are highest risk for causing a delay or error in the sukuk issuance? List the top 3 risk items and explain in one sentence why each one is problematic." → Copy the output into your working file.
  2. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 2
    Build a Shariah Compliance Checklist
    Ask Copilot to generate a clear, structured Shariah compliance checklist for a standard Murabahah sukuk issuance. It should cover: Aqad, Asset Screening, Profit Rate, Documentation, and Governance. Use this prompt: "Create a 15-item Shariah compliance checklist for a Murabahah sukuk issuance. Format as a table with columns: Item, Category, Required Documentation, Status (blank for filling in)." → Paste the checklist into your working file.
  3. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 3
    Draft the Improvement Memo
    Use Copilot to draft a short memo from the Head of Islamic Finance to the Shariah Advisory Committee. The memo should acknowledge the recent errors and their root cause (manual process, fragmented documents), propose one specific process improvement (for example, a standardised digital checklist reviewed before submission), and commit to a quality gate before the next issuance. Keep it under 200 words. Respectful and professional in tone. → Copy into your working file.
