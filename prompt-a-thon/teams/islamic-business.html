Team 2 · Islamic Finance

Islamic Finance Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Shariah Checklist Crunch"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Islamic Finance division manages the purchase of Islamic mortgage assets and the issuance of Shariah-compliant sukuk. The division works closely with the Shariah Advisory Committee to ensure all products meet Islamic finance principles. Every sukuk issuance requires a full Shariah compliance package, and preparing it is taking too long.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Every sukuk issuance requires the team to manually cross-check 140+ Shariah compliance items across Arabic fatwas and English prospectuses. This takes 3 to 4 working days. In 2 of the last 6 issuances, the Shariah Advisory Committee found discrepancies during final review, causing 3 to 5 day delays and extra legal costs.

The team needs a faster, more reliable way to identify the highest-risk review items, standardise the checklist, and improve pre-submission quality before the next issuance cycle begins.

📧 Email Thread

From: Farhan Ibrahim (Head of Islamic Finance) To: CFO; Chief Strategy Officer Subject: Sukuk Issuance Timeline: Shariah Compliance Process Constraint We have four sukuk issuances scheduled for 2026. At the current pace, preparing the Shariah compliance package for each issuance takes 12 to 15 team hours over 4 working days. The last-minute corrections in February and April each cost us 4 days of delay. The Shariah Advisory Committee has been patient but has communicated their expectation that we improve our pre-submission quality. We need to work differently.

🎯 Your Tasks

Task 1 is your must-do (~30 min). Tasks 2 & 3 are bonus stretches if you finish early. Quality > quantity.

  1. Identify the Risk Items⏱️ ~25 min
    The scenario documents hide a few Shariah compliance gaps. What's missing, what feels inconsistent, and what could create risk later? Deliver: a concise summary of the top compliance concerns.
  2. Build a Shariah Compliance Checklist⏱️ ~25 min
    Your team needs to communicate Shariah compliance requirements for Islamic mortgage products to internal stakeholders. Make it clear enough for non-experts, but precise enough to be trusted. Deliver: a short guidance note or internal memo.
  3. Draft the Improvement Memo⏱️ ~25 min
    HHFC needs to communicate Islamic product options better to lenders. Think about language, format, and what lenders genuinely need to understand quickly. Deliver: 3 concrete recommendations.
