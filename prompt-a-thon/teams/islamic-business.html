Islamic Finance Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Shariah Submission Bottleneck"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Islamic Finance division manages the purchase of Islamic mortgage assets (Islamic PWR and PWOR), the issuance of Shariah-compliant sukuk to fund Islamic operations, and maintains the corporation's Islamic Mortgage Guarantee Programme (MGP-i). The division works closely with the Shariah Advisory Committee to ensure all products and transactions meet Islamic finance principles.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Every sukuk issuance requires manually cross-checking 140+ Shariah compliance items across Arabic-language fatwas, English prospectuses, and prior issuance precedents. In 2 of the last 6 issuances, the Shariah Advisory Committee identified discrepancies — causing 3–5 business day delays and increased legal costs.

The process is entirely manual. The Islamic Finance team uses a printed checklist and highlights documents with physical pens. Cross-referencing between Arabic source texts and English translations takes 3–4 working days. As HHFC scales its sukuk programme to four new structures in 2026, this manual process threatens to become the binding constraint.

📧 Executive Email

From: Farhan Ibrahim (Head of Islamic Finance) To: CFO; Chief Strategy Officer Subject: Sukuk Issuance Timeline — Shariah Compliance Process Constraint We have four sukuk issuances scheduled for 2026. At the current pace, preparing the Shariah compliance package for each issuance consumes 12–15 team-hours over 4 working days. The last-minute corrections in February and April cost us approximately 4 days of delay per issuance. The Shariah Advisory Committee has been patient, but they have communicated their expectation that we improve our pre-submission quality controls. I need us to have a serious conversation about how we approach this process differently. At current volume, we are approaching a point where the Shariah compliance preparation timeline becomes the binding constraint on our entire issuance schedule.
