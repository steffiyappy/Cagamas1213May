Treasury & Capital Markets Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Investor Intelligence Gap"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Treasury & Capital Markets division raises the capital needed to fund mortgage purchases — through conventional bonds, sukuk, and commercial paper issued 8–12 times a year. The team also manages all investor communications around each issuance.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Every issuance requires a full set of investor documents built from scratch — each cycle consumes 4 days of team capacity. During those 4 days the team can't monitor markets or respond to investors. Two issuances in the past year were priced 8–12bps worse than target because rate movements happened while the team was heads-down producing documents. Investors have also called HHFC's Q&A responses "generic."

The team needs a faster way to turn issuance data into sharper investor communications so they can spend less time formatting documents and more time shaping the market narrative.

🎯 Your Tasks

Three focused Copilot tasks. Each one produces a real output you'll present at the end. Aim for ~25 minutes per task.

  1. Spot the Pattern⏱️ ~25 min
    Open Copilot Chat. Paste the issuance log and ask: "Analyse this issuance log. Is there a pattern between documentation days, queries answered same day, and rate outcome versus target? Write 3 key findings in plain language that a CFO could understand in 30 seconds." → Copy findings into your working file.
  2. Draft an Investor FAQ⏱️ ~25 min
    Use Copilot to write a 5-question Investor FAQ for an upcoming Cagamas Notes issuance. Questions should cover: what HHFC does, why it is issuing now, the credit quality, the use of proceeds, and the yield expectation. Prompt to use: "Write a 5-question Investor FAQ for a new Cagamas Notes issuance by Harmoni Housing Finance Corporation. HHFC is Malaysia's national mortgage corporation with an AAA local rating. The notes will fund new mortgage purchases. Write in a professional, investor-friendly tone." → Paste FAQ into your working file.
  3. Write the Market Commentary Paragraph⏱️ ~25 min
    Use Copilot to draft a 1-paragraph market commentary for investors — covering the current Malaysian interest rate environment and why now is a good time to invest in HHFC notes. Prompt: "Write a concise 1-paragraph market commentary for bond investors about the Malaysian fixed income market in 2025–2026, and why a highly rated quasi-sovereign issuer like HHFC represents a compelling investment at this time." → Paste into your working file. This will be the opening section of your investor brief.
