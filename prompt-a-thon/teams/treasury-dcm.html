Treasury & Capital Markets Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Funding Narrative Gap"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Treasury & Capital Markets division is responsible for the corporation's funding strategy — raising capital through bond issuances, sukuk, and commercial paper. The team manages investor relations, prepares all investor-facing documentation for each issuance, and monitors market conditions to time funding activities optimally. HHFC issues 8–12 instruments per year.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Every issuance requires rebuilding investor documents from scratch — consuming 4 days of team capacity during which the team cannot monitor markets. On two occasions in the past 12 months, rate movements during the documentation period resulted in issuances priced 8–12bps less favourably than they could have been.

There is no template library and no content repository. Investor feedback from the most recent roadshow was pointed: Q&A responses were described as "generic" and "not responsive to current market questions." One fund manager noted that HHFC's peers were sending pre-issuance market intelligence briefs that HHFC had never produced.
