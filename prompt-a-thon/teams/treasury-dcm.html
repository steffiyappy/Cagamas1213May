Team 3 · Treasury & Capital Markets

Treasury & Capital Markets Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Investor Intelligence Gap"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Treasury & Capital Markets division raises the capital needed to fund mortgage purchases through conventional bonds, sukuk, and commercial paper issued 8 to 12 times a year. The team also manages all investor communications around each issuance.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Every issuance requires a full set of investor documents built from scratch, and each cycle consumes 4 days of team capacity. During those 4 days the team can't monitor markets or respond to investors. Two issuances in the past year were priced 8 to 12 bps worse than target because rate movements happened while the team was heads-down producing documents. Investors have also called HHFC's Q&A responses "generic."

The team needs a faster way to turn issuance data into sharper investor communications so they can spend less time formatting documents and more time shaping the market narrative.

🎯 Your Tasks

Task 1 is your must-do (~30 min). Tasks 2 & 3 are bonus stretches if you finish early. Quality > quantity.

  1. Spot the Pattern⏱️ ~25 min
    Your treasury team has a mountain of market data. What are the 3 signals that matter most right now, and what do they mean for decisions? Deliver: a concise market briefing summary.
  2. Draft an Investor FAQ⏱️ ~25 min
    Investors are asking questions about HHFC's latest issuance. Think about what builds confidence: clarity, credibility, and the right level of detail. Deliver: a draft investor communication.
  3. Write the Market Commentary Paragraph⏱️ ~25 min
    The CFO wants the bond programme explained without jargon or fluff. What are the 3 messages that will land best in an investor meeting? Deliver: 3 CFO-ready talking points.
