Finance & Reporting Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Board Pack Bottleneck"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Finance division produces the monthly Management Accounts Pack — reviewed by EXCO and the Board every cycle. The pack is HHFC's single most important internal document. The process of producing it is broken.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Producing the monthly Management Accounts Pack takes 6 working days. Finance spends 5 of those days collecting and reconciling data from 14 cost centres — in different Excel formats, with inconsistent definitions. By the time numbers are clean, there is one day left to write Board commentary. The CFO has returned packs 3 times this year requesting more substantive analysis. On two occasions, wrong figures went to EXCO.

The team needs a cleaner submission process and better commentary so the Board receives accurate numbers with clear insight, not just late reconciliations.

🎯 Your Tasks

Three focused Copilot tasks. Each one produces a real output you'll present at the end. Aim for ~25 minutes per task.

  1. Find the Reconciliation Failures⏱️ ~25 min
    The numbers don't explain themselves. Spot the most significant variances and decide what deserves escalation. Deliver: a variance analysis summary in bullet form.
  2. Draft the Variance Commentary⏱️ ~25 min
    A CFO commentary should do more than restate the numbers. What is the story behind this period, and how do you sound confident, transparent, and forward-looking at once? Deliver: a draft management commentary paragraph.
  3. Draft the Process Fix Email⏱️ ~25 min
    HHFC's board reporting process needs sharper discipline. Based on what you've seen, what should change first, and why would it matter? Deliver: 3 actionable board reporting recommendations.
