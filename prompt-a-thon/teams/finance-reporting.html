Finance & Reporting Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Management Pack Marathon"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Finance division manages financial planning, Group performance reporting, and Board-level financial communication. Every month, the team produces the Management Accounts Pack — reviewed by EXCO and the Board of Directors. The pack is HHFC's single most important internal document, and the process of producing it is consuming resources that should be spent on analysis and insight.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Producing HHFC's monthly Management Accounts Pack takes 6 working days — nearly a third of the working month. Data from 14 cost centres arrives in different formats. On two occasions, incorrect figures were included in the pack circulated to EXCO. In one case, an EXCO member spotted a discrepancy during the meeting itself.

The team spends 80% of their time collecting and reconciling data, leaving only one day to write financial commentary. The CFO has returned packs three times in the past year requesting more substantive analysis, with the message: "This is a process design failure, not an individual performance issue."

📧 CFO Email

From: Mei Lin Chong (CFO) To: Head of Finance & Reporting Subject: Management Pack Quality — Enough I need to be direct. The last three packs have had commentary that reads like it was written in 30 minutes. Because it was. This is not an individual performance issue — it is a process design failure. We are spending 80% of our time collecting and reconciling data and 20% on the thing that matters: telling leadership what the numbers mean. I want a proposal by end of month for how we fundamentally redesign this process. Not incremental improvement. A new model. We have the tools. We need to use them differently.

🎯 Your Tasks

  1. Review the adjustment log and identify the root causes of recurring reconciliation failures. Assess whether HHFC's current month-end process is structurally capable of producing timely, accurate financial commentary.
  2. Redesign the month-end reporting process to reduce the data collection and reconciliation phase from 5 days to 2 days — describe exactly what changes and how Copilot supports the new model.
  3. Using the adjustment log data and the CFO's directive as context, draft a sample Management Discussion & Analysis commentary for HHFC's Q2 results — show the Board what good looks like.
