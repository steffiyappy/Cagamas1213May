Risk & Compliance Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Regulatory Radar Problem"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Risk & Compliance division manages the corporation's regulatory compliance obligations, risk frameworks, and internal controls. The team monitors updates from Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM), the Securities Commission (SC), and international Islamic finance bodies including AAOIFI. The division produces quarterly compliance reports for the Board Risk Committee and manages HHFC's Risk Register.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: BNM, SC, and AAOIFI collectively issue ~8 regulatory circulars per month. The team spends 14 hours per week manually tracking these in Excel. Two minor compliance breaches in the past year were only identified during quarterly review — meaning HHFC was technically non-compliant for up to 3 months. HHFC received verbal guidance from BNM examiners on strengthening its monitoring process.

Impact assessments are shallow: the team logs whether a regulation is "relevant" or "not relevant" without structured analysis of what operational changes may be required. "Partial" and "Not Started" entries sit in the log for months. The BNM examiner's Q3 visit is approaching.

📧 CRO Email

From: Robert Tan Swee Hin (Chief Risk Officer) To: Head of Compliance; Risk Team Subject: BNM Examiner Visit — Q3 Readiness The examiner will be on-site in Q3. I have reviewed our compliance monitoring log and I am not satisfied with the depth of our impact assessments. "Partial" and "Not Started" entries for regulations that have been in our log for 3 months are not acceptable. We received verbal guidance last year about this. I do not want a repeat. I need a full impact assessment completed for every open item before the end of this month. I also want a proposal for how we redesign the monitoring process to prevent this from happening again. We need real-time awareness, not quarterly catch-up.

🎯 Your Tasks

  1. Review the regulatory monitoring log and produce a risk prioritisation assessment — which open items pose the greatest risk to HHFC and why? What does the pattern of "Not Started" and "Partial" assessments tell you about the process?
  2. Redesign HHFC's regulatory monitoring process to move from reactive quarterly assessment to proactive real-time awareness — describe specifically how Copilot tools can support the team in reading, summarising, and assessing new circulars at speed.
  3. Draft a complete impact assessment for one of the "Not Started" items in the log (choose the one you assess as highest risk), demonstrating the depth of analysis that the Chief Risk Officer expects.
