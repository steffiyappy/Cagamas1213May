Risk & Compliance Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Compliance Catch-Up"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Risk & Compliance division monitors regulatory updates from BNM, the Securities Commission (SC), and AAOIFI, and manages HHFC's compliance obligations. The team tracks ~8 new regulatory circulars per month. Currently, they're doing this in Excel — and it's not keeping pace.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: The team spends 14 hours per week manually reading, categorising, and logging regulatory material. Impact assessments are shallow — many items are logged as "Partial" or "Not Started." Two compliance breaches in the past year were only found during the quarterly review, meaning HHFC was non-compliant for up to 3 months. The BNM examiner is visiting in Q3.

The immediate priority is to rank the open items, deepen one assessment, and show leadership there is now a clear control process before the examiner arrives.

🎯 Your Tasks

Three focused Copilot tasks. Each one produces a real output you'll present at the end. Aim for ~25 minutes per task.

  1. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 1
    Prioritise the Risks
    Open Copilot Chat. Paste the regulatory monitoring log and ask: "Based on this regulatory monitoring log, rank the top 3 open items by risk to HHFC. For each one, write 2 sentences: why it is high priority, and what HHFC needs to do about it immediately. Consider that HHFC is a mortgage corporation regulated by BNM, and a BNM examiner is visiting in Q3." → Copy the prioritisation into your working file.
  2. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 2
    Draft an Impact Assessment
    Choose the highest-risk open item from Task 1. Use Copilot to draft a brief impact assessment for it. Prompt: "Write a preliminary compliance impact assessment for [item name] issued by [BNM/SC/AAOIFI]. HHFC is Malaysia's national mortgage corporation. The assessment should cover: (1) What the regulation requires, (2) How it likely applies to HHFC's operations, (3) What gaps HHFC may have, (4) Recommended next steps. Keep it to 5–6 bullet points. Note it is a preliminary assessment pending legal review." → Paste into your working file.
  3. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 3
    Write the CRO Update
    Use Copilot to draft a brief email update from the Head of Compliance to the CRO. The email should summarise the top 3 open risk items in priority order, confirm what actions have been taken this week, and propose one process change to prevent "Not Started" items accumulating in future (for example, a weekly 30-min triage meeting). Keep it under 200 words — clear and action-oriented. → Copy into your working file.
