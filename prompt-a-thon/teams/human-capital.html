Human Capital Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The People Readiness Sprint"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Human Capital division manages talent, learning & development, and HR policy for 350 staff. Harmoni Vision 2027 demands new digital and AI skills across all functions, but the current upskilling programme is significantly behind target.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: All 350 staff were supposed to complete an AI upskilling programme by end of 2025. With 6 months remaining, completion sits at 41%. Training records are managed in an Excel file that hasn't been updated in 3 weeks. Three high-potential employees ("Ready Now" for promotion) left in H1, citing unclear career paths. HR also receives 23 policy queries per week, and the Policy Manual was last updated 14 months ago.

The CHRO needs a focused recovery plan that improves completion momentum, shows care for staff development, and frees the HR team from repetitive policy questions.

📧 Email Thread

From: Amirah Zulkifli (CHRO) To: Head of Learning & Development; HR Business Partners Subject: Talent Crisis: We Need to Act Now Three "Ready Now" promotions walked out the door in H1. The exit interview data is consistent: unclear career paths, inadequate development support, and the feeling that HHFC is not investing in its people. We also have a training completion crisis. 41% is not a number we can present to the Board with confidence. I need a credible action plan with data-driven insights, a redesigned L&D approach, and a proposal for self-service policy queries.

🎯 Your Tasks

Task 1 is your must-do (~30 min). Tasks 2 & 3 are bonus stretches if you finish early. Quality > quantity.

  1. Spot Who's Falling Behind⏱️ ~25 min
    The HR data reveals some uncomfortable truths. Where is the pain building, and what is most at risk if nothing changes? Deliver: a summary of the top 3 people-related concerns.
  2. Draft the Nudge Email⏱️ ~25 min
    Employees need to hear from HR in a way that feels human, honest, and helpful. Focus on the most pressing concern in your scenario and get the tone right. Deliver: a short HR communication draft.
  3. Write the Retention Action Summary⏱️ ~25 min
    Talent is HHFC's most important asset, and retention takes more than pay. What would actually help people stay, grow, and feel engaged here? Deliver: 3 concrete talent retention recommendations.
