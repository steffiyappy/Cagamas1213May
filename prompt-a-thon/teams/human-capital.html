Human Capital Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The Talent Readiness Gap"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Human Capital division manages talent acquisition, learning & development, HR policy, performance management, and employee relations for the corporation's 350 staff. Harmoni Vision 2027 has placed unprecedented demands on the workforce — requiring new digital literacy, AI skills, and cross-functional capabilities that the current workforce was not originally hired for.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: Harmoni Vision 2027 requires all 350 staff to complete a structured digital and AI upskilling programme by end of 2025. With 6 months remaining, training completion sits at just 41%. Three high-potential employees rated "Ready Now" for promotion left in H1, citing "lack of clear career path" in exit interviews.

Training records are maintained in a shared Excel workbook last updated 3 weeks ago. HR receives an average of 23 policy queries per week, many from managers who cannot locate answers in existing documents. The team spends approximately 8 hours per week answering repeat questions that a well-designed self-service resource could handle.

📧 CHRO Email

From: Amirah Zulkifli (CHRO) To: Head of Learning & Development; HR Business Partners Subject: Talent Crisis — We Need to Act Now Three "Ready Now" promotions walked out the door in H1. The exit interview data is consistent: unclear career paths, inadequate development support, and the feeling that HHFC is not investing in its people. We also have a training completion crisis. 41% is not a number we can present to the Board with confidence. I need a credible action plan by end of month. Not a list of good intentions. I need data-driven insights on where we are losing people, a redesigned L&D tracking approach that gives us real visibility, and a proposal for how we self-service our policy queries so the team can focus on strategic talent work.

🎯 Your Tasks

  1. Analyse the training completion data and identify which departments and programmes are most at risk of missing their deadlines. What does the data tell you about where the L&D strategy is failing?
  2. Design a smarter approach to tracking and improving training completion — describe how Copilot tools could give L&D real-time visibility and help personalise learning nudges for staff who are falling behind.
  3. Draft a "Talent Retention Action Plan" for the CHRO to present to the Board Staff Compensation & Organisation Committee — addressing both the completion crisis and the high-potential attrition pattern.
