Human Capital Pack

⚡ Challenge: "The People Readiness Sprint"

🏢 About Your Division

HHFC's Human Capital division manages talent, learning & development, and HR policy for 350 staff. Harmoni Vision 2027 demands new digital and AI skills across all functions — but the current upskilling programme is significantly behind target.

⚠️ The Business Pain Point

Core Problem: All 350 staff were supposed to complete an AI upskilling programme by end of 2025. With 6 months remaining, completion sits at 41%. Training records are managed in an Excel file that hasn't been updated in 3 weeks. Three high-potential employees ("Ready Now" for promotion) left in H1, citing unclear career paths. HR also receives 23 policy queries per week — the Policy Manual was last updated 14 months ago.

The CHRO needs a focused recovery plan that improves completion momentum, shows care for staff development, and frees the HR team from repetitive policy questions.

📧 Email Thread

From: Amirah Zulkifli (CHRO) To: Head of Learning & Development; HR Business Partners Subject: Talent Crisis — We Need to Act Now Three "Ready Now" promotions walked out the door in H1. The exit interview data is consistent: unclear career paths, inadequate development support, and the feeling that HHFC is not investing in its people. 🎯 Your Tasks

Three focused Copilot tasks. Each one produces a real output you'll present at the end. Aim for ~25 minutes per task.

  1. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 1
    Spot Who's Falling Behind
    Open Copilot Chat. Paste the training completion table and ask: "Analyse this training completion log. Which programmes and departments are most at risk of missing their deadlines? Rank the top 3 most urgent situations and explain in one sentence why each one is a problem." → Copy into your working file.
  2. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 2
    Draft the Nudge Email
    Use Copilot to write a motivating (not threatening) email from the CHRO to staff in the departments with the lowest completion rates. The email should acknowledge that training on top of daily work is hard, remind them why Harmoni Vision 2027 matters for their career, and give a clear call to action — complete one module this week. Keep it under 150 words. Warm, personal, and energising. → Copy into your working file.
  3. ⏱️ ~25 min · Task 3
    Write the Retention Action Summary
    Use Copilot to write a 3-point Talent Retention Action Summary for the CHRO to present at the next Board HR Committee meeting. Each point should address one of: (1) training completion recovery, (2) career path clarity for high-potentials, (3) self-service policy access to free up HR time. Each point = problem + proposed action + expected outcome. Keep each point to 3 sentences. → Paste into your working file and prepare to present these 3 points to the room.

  • Be specific in your prompts — the more context you give, the better the output
