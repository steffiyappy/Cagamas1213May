🎯 Your Tasks (Same Shape for Every Theme)

Task 1 is your must-do (~30 min). Tasks 2 & 3 are bonus stretches if you finish early. Quality > quantity. Use the GCSE framework (Goal · Context · Source · Expectation) for every prompt.

  1. Diagnose the Problem ⏱️ ~30 min · Must-Do
    Use Copilot to analyse the data, emails, or context tied to your chosen theme. Deliver: a sharp 4-bullet summary that names the root cause, the people affected, and the cost of inaction.
  2. Draft the Communication ⭐ Bonus
    Pick the audience your theme demands — a lender, an investor, the CFO, BNM, or your team. Deliver: one polished email or message under 200 words that actually moves the situation forward.
  3. Build the Leadership Brief ⭐ Stretch
    You're presenting to EXCO in 30 minutes. Deliver: 3 leadership-ready talking points framed as Problem · Cause · Fix. Make every word earn its place.

💡 Copilot Tips for Today

  • Copilot Chat (Teams or Microsoft365.com): best for analysing data and drafting documents quickly
  • Copilot in Word: use "Draft with Copilot" to generate longer documents, then edit
  • Copilot in Outlook: use "Draft with Copilot" to write professional emails fast
  • Paste your data directly into the chat so Copilot can read tables and lists you copy in
  • Be specific in your prompts: the more context you give, the better the output. Always GCSE.

Power Up: Locked

Ship Task 1 first. Take on bonus tasks only if time allows. Then unlock your Power Up — one prompt that turns your work today into something the team will actually keep using.

✅ Power Up Unlocked! Run this prompt to push your work from "we made it once" to "we'd actually use this again".
⚡
Your Power Up Prompt
In the same Copilot chat where you built your artefact (so it still has context) — or open the file and use Copilot inside it — paste this prompt:
"Take the [email / brief / analysis / table] we just produced together and help us turn it into something we'd actually re-use at Cagamas. Give me: (1) a clean reusable prompt template with bracketed inputs like [lender name] or [reporting period] that anyone on my team could fill in, (2) three specific moments in our work week where this prompt would save real time, and (3) one bold idea to make this 10× more useful — something we didn't think of."
💡 If you've started a new Copilot chat, attach your artefact first using the + / paperclip button, or open the Word/Excel/PowerPoint file and use Copilot from inside it — then paste the prompt and replace the [bracketed type] with what you actually built.
💡 The point isn't a polished demo — it's leaving today with a prompt your team will actually paste into Copilot next Monday.

🎤 Your 5-Minute Demo

  • Slide 1: Which theme you picked (or the one you invented) and why
  • Slide 2: The single best prompt your team wrote (and why it worked)
  • Slide 3: The output — show the actual artefact
  • Slide 4: Where you'd take it next — your reusable prompt + the moments you'd use it
  • Keep it tight. Demo > describe. Show the prompt, show the output, then sit down.
