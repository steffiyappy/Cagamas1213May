Don't have a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence? No problem.
Designate 1 "Driver" per team who has the M365 Copilot licence — they run Copilot on their laptop and screen-share. Everyone else co-designs: read the scenario, suggest prompts, evaluate outputs, and document what worked. The Driver types — the team thinks.
No licensed person at all? Use free Copilot Chat at copilot.microsoft.com (sign in with a personal Microsoft account) — you'll lose Office app integration but can still practise prompting craft.

🎮 How It Works

Five steps. Hack hour, prep, then the stage.

1

Find Your Pack 🔐 ~3 min

Enter your team code to unlock your department's game pack. Pick your Driver.

2

Read the Brief 📋 ~7 min

Skim the HHFC pain point and the data your team has been given. Decide your angle.

3

Hack with Copilot 💡 ~45 min

Tackle 1 main Copilot task as a team. Bonus tasks if you finish early. Document your prompts.

4

Power Up & Prep ⚡ 5 + 30 min

Unlock your hidden Power Up to sharpen the output. Then build your 5-minute pitch deck.
