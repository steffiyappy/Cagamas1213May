  ~45 min  — Hack time (Task 1 must-do; Tasks 2 & 3 bonus if time allows)
   ~5 min  — Power Up reveal and output polish
   --- 60-min hack ends ---

  Assume only 1–2 people per team have a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence.

  Everyone else co-designs prompts, evaluates outputs, and documents.

    — no Office app integration, but prompting craft still applies.

  one of 6 HHFC challenge themes (or invent their own). Two teams may
  pick the same theme — peer judging will compare approaches.
